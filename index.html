01Gas氣動

Flash eliminator消焰器

The slotted steel tube at the muzzle. It breaks up the flash and protects the crown of the barrel; the lug underneath carries a bayonet.槍口末端的開槽鋼管。它打散火焰，保護槍管前端的膛口；下方的凸耳用來安裝刺刀。

Note註The L1A1’s eliminator is longer and fully enclosed than the open-pronged metric FAL type. It also accepts a blank-firing attachment.L1A1 的消焰器比公制 FAL 的長，而且全包式設計，亦可安裝空包彈助退器。

02Gas氣動

Barrel槍管

A rifled steel tube, 554 mm long. The chamber at the rear holds the cartridge; the grooves spin the bullet for stability. The front sight and gas block sit on it.內有膛線的鋼管，長 554 毫米。後端的彈膛容納子彈，膛線令彈頭旋轉穩定。準星與導氣箍都裝在槍管上。

Note註Chambered for 7.62 × 51 mm NATO. The gas port is drilled near the front; everything after it belongs to the gas system.口徑為 7.62×51 毫米北約彈。導氣孔開在槍管前段，之後的事就交給氣動系統。

03Gas氣動

Front sight準星

The post you centre inside the rear aperture. Two steel ears protect it from knocks. Raising or lowering the post changes where the rifle shoots.你透過照門看到的立柱。兩側鋼耳保護它免受碰撞。把準星調高或調低，會改變彈著點。

Note註The foresight is a separate assembly, zeroed with a small tool. Cadets should not adjust it.準星是獨立組件，由裝甲師以專用工具歸零；學員不應自行調整。

04Gas氣動

Gas block導氣箍

A steel collar around the barrel, just ahead of the handguard. It collects gas from the barrel and houses the gas plug, regulator and cylinder. It also anchors the handguard and front sight.包住槍管的鋼箍，位於護木前方。它收集槍管導出的氣體，容納導氣塞、調節器與氣缸，同時固定護木和準星。

Note註The gas block is the front anchor of the whole upper assembly. Its fit affects accuracy as much as the barrel does.導氣箍是整個上組件的前支點。它的裝配精度對準確度的影響，不亞於槍管本身。

Rel.相關 05 06

05Gas氣動

Gas regulator氣體調節器

A graduated collar that decides how much gas reaches the piston. Turning it vents more or less gas, so the action still cycles when the rifle is cold, dirty or hot.帶刻度的套環，決定有多少氣體推動活塞。轉動它可多排或少排一點氣，讓步槍在寒冷、髒污或高溫下仍能循環。

Note註Set by the armourer, not the user. The number on the collar is a reference position, not a “power” setting.由裝甲師設定，不是學員的日常控制。環上的數字是參考位置，不是「火力」設定。

Rel.相關 04 06

06Gas氣動

Gas piston導氣活塞

A short steel rod in the cylinder above the barrel. Gas pushes it back; it strikes the bolt carrier and immediately returns to rest. One push, then it is out of the way.槍管上方氣缸內的一根短鋼桿。氣體把它向後推，它撞一下槍機框便立即回位。只推一次，然後讓開。

Note註Because the piston does not travel with the carrier, the FAL is a short-stroke system. A spring around the piston returns it.活塞不隨槍機框全程移動，所以 FAL 屬於「短行程」氣動系統。活塞周圍的彈簧負責復位。

Rel.相關 04 10

07Body槍身

Handguard護木

Two mirror halves that cover the barrel and gas cylinder, so your support hand is not on bare steel. They also protect the barrel from knocks.兩片左右對稱的外殼，覆蓋槍管與氣缸，讓支撐手不用直接握著熱鋼。它也保護槍管免受碰撞。

Note註Early rifles used walnut; later L1A1s used Maranyl, a nylon and fibreglass composite. The halves are not interchangeable left-to-right.早期用胡桃木，後期 L1A1 改用 Maranyl（尼龍與玻璃纖維複合材料）。左右兩片不能互換。

Rel.相關 02 04

08Body槍身

Carry handle提把

A folding handle on the barrel group. It lets the rifle be carried comfortably and keeps the hand away from a hot barrel.槍管組上的摺疊提把，方便攜行，也讓手遠離熾熱的槍管。

Note註It is spring-loaded and folds flat. It is not a sight, and it should not be used to steady the rifle.內有彈簧，可平貼護木。它不是瞄具，也不應用來穩定射擊。

Rel.相關 04 07

09Action機件

Top cover頂蓋

The long lid over the receiver. It keeps dirt out of the boltway, and on some variants it carries the rear sight or an optical-sight rail.機匣上方的長蓋，阻止泥沙進入槍機槽；部分版本也在上面安裝照門或光學瞄具導軌。

Note註The L1A1 added retaining tabs at the rear so the cover cannot creep forward and lose the zero of a SUIT sight.L1A1 在後端加了固定凸耳，防止頂蓋前移，避免 SUIT 瞄具失去歸零。

Rel.相關 10 12

10Action機件

Bolt carrier槍機框

The moving frame inside the receiver. The gas piston strikes it; its cam track lifts and lowers the bolt; it carries the charging handle and compresses the return spring.機匣內移動的框架。導氣活塞撞擊它；它的凸輪槽抬起和放下槍機；它帶動拉機柄，並壓縮復進簧。

Note註The carrier does not lock the rifle. It only moves the bolt; the bolt itself locks into the receiver.槍機框本身不閉鎖，只負責移動槍機；真正閉鎖的是槍機。

Rel.相關 11 12

11Action機件

Bolt槍機

The part that seals the chamber. Its rear tips down into a recess in the receiver floor and bears on the locking shoulder. It carries the extractor and the firing pin.密封彈膛的零件。尾部向下傾入機匣底部的凹槽，抵住閉鎖肩。它帶著抽殼鈎和擊針。

Note註The L1A1 locks by tipping the bolt’s tail down, not by rotating the bolt like an AR-15. The locking shoulder is a hardened insert in the receiver.L1A1 是「槍機尾下傾」閉鎖，不像 AR-15 那樣旋轉槍機。閉鎖肩是機匣內的硬化嵌件。

Rel.相關 10 14

12Action機件

Return spring復進簧

A long coil spring behind the carrier, housed in the butt of the rifle. It stores the energy of the recoil and drives the carrier, bolt and next round back into battery.槍機框後方的長彈簧，藏在槍托內。它吸收後座的能量，再把槍機框、槍機和下一發子彈推回膛室。

Note註A weak or bent spring causes failures to return to battery. It is captive under the top cover; do not kink it.彈簧變弱或彎曲會導致復進不到位。它由頂蓋壓住，不要扭折。

13Action機件

Charging handle拉機柄

The folding handle on the left of the receiver. Pulling it back drags the carrier and bolt rearward, cocking the hammer and letting you clear the chamber.機匣左側的摺疊手柄。向後拉會帶動槍機框和槍機後退，令擊錘待發，並可清空彈膛。

Note註The L1A1’s handle stays still when the rifle fires, unlike the metric FAL’s reciprocating handle. Keep your hand clear of it anyway.L1A1 的拉機柄在射擊時保持不動，這是它與公制 FAL 的分別之一。操作時仍要讓手遠離它。

Rel.相關 10 14

14Action機件

Receiver機匣

The forged steel body that holds the barrel, the bolt group, the magazine and the trigger housing. It contains the locking recess and the ejection port. It is the spine of the rifle.鍛鋼製的主體，容納槍管、槍機組、彈匣和扳機座。內有閉鎖凹槽和拋殼口。它是整支槍的脊骨。

Note註Inch and metric FAL receivers look alike but differ in threads, cuts and small parts. The L1A1’s lightening cuts are its own pattern.英制與公制 FAL 的機匣外表相似，但螺紋、切削和細件不同。L1A1 的減重切削是它自己的樣式。

Rel.相關 11 16

15Feed供彈

Magazine彈匣

A 20-round steel box. A spring pushes the rounds up; the top round is stripped off by the bolt as it closes. The catch at the front holds it in the magazine well.20 發鋼製盒式彈匣。彈簧把子彈向上推，槍機前進時把最上面一發推入彈膛。前方的卡榫把它固定在彈匣井內。

Note註The inch magazine has a brazed front lug. Metric magazines may sometimes fit, but inch magazines will not fit a metric FAL. Treat a loaded magazine as live.英制彈匣前方有焊接凸耳；公制彈匣有時可用，但英制彈匣不能插入公制 FAL。裝滿的彈匣一律當作已上膛。

Rel.相關 14 16

16Action機件

Trigger group扳機組

The lower housing that holds the trigger, hammer, sear and change lever. The trigger releases the hammer; the hammer strikes the firing pin; the sear catches the hammer again as the carrier moves back.下部的扳機座，容納扳機、擊錘、阻鐵和快慢機。扳機釋放擊錘，擊錘打擊擊針；槍機框後退時，阻鐵再次扣住擊錘。

Note註The housing hinges down at the rear for maintenance. The change lever has only S (safe) and R (repeat) — no automatic setting.扳機座在後方鉸接，可向下打開保養。快慢機只有 S（保險）和 R（單發），沒有全自動。

Rel.相關 11 15

17Body槍身

Buttstock槍托

The shoulder piece. It holds the return-spring guide, sets the length of pull and gives the rifle its balance. The butt trap holds a cleaning kit and oil bottle.抵肩的部分。它容納復進簧導桿，決定握持長度，也影響全槍平衡。托底板內的暗格可放清潔工具和油壺。

Note註L1A1 stocks were made in four lengths, plus a short “Arctic” pattern. The buttplate is a separate casting.L1A1 槍托有四種長度，另有供極寒衣著使用的短版。托底板是獨立鑄件。

Rel.相關 12 14

18Body槍身

Rear sight照門

The folding aperture on the receiver. You look through the hole and centre the front post; the leaf folds forward when not in use. Range and windage are set on its ramp and screws.機匣上的摺疊覘孔。你透過小孔看出去，把準星置中；不用時可向前摺下。距離與風偏由斜軌和螺絲調整。

Note註The standard L1A1 uses a folding aperture. The Hythe and SUIT sights were separate kits, not fitted to every rifle.標準 L1A1 用摺疊覘孔；Hythe 與 SUIT 是另外加裝的瞄具，不是每支槍都有。

03

The cycle運作循環

Six moments, one shot. Step through them, or let the page walk you from feed to lock, fire, unlock, extract and return.六個瞬間，一發子彈。逐步觀看，或讓頁面帶你由供彈、閉鎖、擊發、解鎖、抽殼到復進。

Semi-automatic: one press, one shot. The L1A1 has no automatic setting; the change lever is S (safe) or R (repeat).半自動：每次扣扳機只發射一發。L1A1 沒有全自動模式；快慢機只有 S（保險）與 R（單發）。

Lineage沿革

Designed in Belgium by Dieudonné Saive and Ernest Vervier between 1947 and 1953, the FAL was adopted by the United Kingdom in 1954 as the L1A1. It was built to imperial measurements by Enfield, BSA and the Royal Ordnance Factories. Australia built its own at Lithgow from 1959; Canada fielded the C1 and C1A1.FAL 由比利時的 Dieudonné Saive 與 Ernest Vervier 於 1947 至 1953 年間設計。英國在 1954 年採用，成為 L1A1，由 Enfield、BSA 及皇家兵工廠按英制尺寸生產。澳洲自 1959 年起在 Lithgow 生產；加拿大則採用 C1 與 C1A1。

What makes it “inch pattern”何謂「英制規格」

  • S / R only. The change lever has safe and repeat; there is no automatic setting.只有 S／R。快慢機設保險與單發，沒有全自動。
  • Folding cocking handle on the left, stationary when firing.左側摺疊拉機柄，射擊時保持不動。
  • Sand cuts in the receiver and bolt group give dirt somewhere to go.排沙槽設於機匣與槍機組，讓沙塵有路可走。
  • Folding trigger guard for use with Arctic mitts.可摺式扳機護圈，方便戴極寒手套操作。
  • Enclosed, slotted flash eliminator with a bayonet lug.全包式開槽消焰器，附刺刀座。
  • Retaining tabs on the top cover; no automatic bolt hold-open.頂蓋固定凸耳；取消自動槍機後定。

Metric and inch parts can look interchangeable and are not. Do not mix them.公制與英制零件外表可能相似，但並不通用。切勿混用。

05

A rifle is not a prop.步槍不是道具。

These rules come before any page, any diagram, any quiz. Your instructor’s orders come before this website.以下規則優先於任何頁面、圖解或測驗。導師的指示，優先於本網站。

  1. 01

    Treat every rifle as loaded — even after you have checked it.把每支槍都當作已上膛——即使你剛剛檢查過。

  2. 02

    Handle a rifle only when an instructor is present and has given the order.只可在導師在場並下達指示時接觸步槍。

  3. 03

    Keep the muzzle pointed in a safe direction. Keep your finger off the trigger until told otherwise.槍口永遠指向安全方向。未獲指示前，手指不要放在扳機上。

  4. 04

    Your unit’s range orders come first. This page is a study aid, not a drill.所屬單位的射擊守則優先。本頁只是學習輔助，不是操練指令。

  5. 05

    Never modify, repair or disassemble a live firearm. Ask your instructor.切勿改裝、修理或拆解可發射的槍械。有疑問，請教導師。

If you are unsure, stop. Ask.如有疑問，立即停止，並請教導師。
